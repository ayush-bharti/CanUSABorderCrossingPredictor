Understanding the Patterns

These visualizations show historical patterns in border crossing wait times at the Blaine Peace Arch crossing between Vancouver and Seattle.

Key factors that influence wait times include:

  • Time of Day: Peak travel hours typically result in longer wait times.
  • Day of Week: Weekends and holidays generally have higher traffic volume.
  • Season: Summer months and holiday seasons experience increased cross-border travel.
  • Special Events: Concerts, sports events, and conferences can cause traffic spikes.
  • Weather Conditions: Severe weather can impact crossing times.

Our prediction model takes these patterns into account to provide accurate wait time forecasts.
