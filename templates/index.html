About the Predictor

This tool predicts wait times at the Blaine Peace Arch border crossing between Vancouver and Seattle using Facebook's Prophet forecasting model.

The model takes into account:

  • Historical patterns by hour, day of week, and month
  • Holiday effects for both US and Canada
  • Current wait time (if provided)

For more detailed visualizations of historical patterns, visit the Visualize page.
